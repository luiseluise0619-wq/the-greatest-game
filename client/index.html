      HOW A ROUND GOES

      • Roles are secret. Sheriff, Deputies, Outlaws, one Renegade. Nobody is told who anybody is.
      • You get one thread to pull. A Deputy learns two names, one of which is the Sheriff. An Outlaw recognises a single accomplice.
      • Kills only name the killer if somebody watched it happen. Everything else is a rumour and a location.
      • The dead reveal their role. Every body is evidence — for whoever is still standing.
      • The Sheriff may pin on the star for real armour and a permanent target. Or stay a stranger.
      • Two cards are dealt to you and never shown to anyone. None of them shoot — they edit what the town is allowed to know: who witnessed a kill, whose boots left prints, who wears the star.
